# Privacy Policy for WC Runner
 
*Effective date: August 10, 2026*
 
This Privacy Policy describes how WC Runner ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile game. WC Runner is developed by Lukas Obertas, based in Slovakia.
 
## 1. No Personal Data Collection
 
WC Runner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App does not require account creation and does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. We do not use any advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
 
## 2. Locally Stored Data
 
The App may save gameplay data (such as high scores, progress, and settings) directly on your device. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed automatically if you uninstall the App.
 
## 3. Third-Party Services
 
WC Runner does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or data collection services. If this changes in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be revised before that update is released, and the "Effective date" above will be updated accordingly.
 
## 4. Children's Privacy
 
WC Runner is suitable for a general audience. Since the App collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).
 
## 5. Data Security
 
As no personal data is collected or transmitted by the App, there is no server-side storage of personal information associated with your use of WC Runner.
 
## 6. Your Rights (EU/EEA Users)
 
Because WC Runner does not collect or process personal data, there is no personal data held by us that you would need to access, correct, or delete.
 
## 7. Changes to This Policy
 
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
