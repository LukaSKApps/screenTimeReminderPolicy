# Privacy Policy for WC Runner

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WC Runner Developer handles information in the **WC Runner** mobile application (the "App"). The App is designed to work **entirely on your device**. I do not operate any servers for the App and I do not receive or collect personal data from you.

---

## Summary (No personal data collected)
- I **do not collect, store, or share** any personal data with me or third parties.
- The App **does not transmit** your data off your device.
- There are **no third‑party SDKs**, **no analytics**, and **no advertising** in the App.

---

## Information processed on your device
To provide its core functionality, the App stores local gameplay data such as **high scores, progress, and settings**. This information is saved **only on your device's local storage** and is used solely inside the App to display your scores and progress. This information is **not linked to your identity**, and is **never sent** to me or to others.

---

## What I do **not** collect
The App does **not** collect or share:
- Names, emails, phone numbers, or account data
- Contacts, calendar, photos, files, messages, or call logs
- Precise location, health, financial, or other sensitive information
- Advertising identifiers or analytics identifiers

---

## Permissions
The App does **not** request sensitive runtime permissions such as location, contacts, microphone, camera, or SMS. It does not require any special permissions beyond what is needed to run the game itself.

You can review or manage any App permissions in your device settings at any time.

---

## Data retention and deletion (on device)
All high scores, progress, and settings are stored **only on your device**. You can delete this data at any time by **clearing the App's storage** in Android Settings, or by **uninstalling** the App.

---

## Children's privacy
This App **may be used by children**. The App does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data from any user, including children.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about on‑device data, you can uninstall the App at any time to remove all on‑device data.

Because no personal data is collected, parental consent is **not required** for this App's operation. If the App changes to collect personal data in the future, the Privacy Policy will be updated and, where required by law, consent will be obtained.

---

## Changes to this policy
I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When I do, I will update the **Effective date** above and make the new version available in the App and/or on the project page.
